# Universal Chiral BV and Einstein Completion

Raeez Lorgat

This book studies mixed boundary operations, derived centres, deformation algebras, and Batalin–Vilkovisky theory. Supplied boundary data define compatible modules and their coherent actions. Local field complexes, pairings, and invariant orientations give classical and algebraic quantum constructions under stated hypotheses. The gravitational comparison relates flat Cartan connections to Einstein solutions on the invertible-coframe locus and identifies the further physical data required.

A holomorphic collision and a transverse ordered composition use different coefficient operations. An interface specifies how their actions interact. The complete mixed datum records a chiral Lie algebra, two associative algebras, an interface bimodule, a Lie comparison, and a continuous mixed distributive law. These data determine compatible modules and their coherent endomorphisms.

The deformation problem varies the operations and their compatibility maps together. A local realization of this deformation problem requires a sheaf of field complexes and a comparison with its algebraic deformation algebra. Once that realization is supplied, shifted cotangent transgression constructs an action and its classical master equation. A compatible invariant orientation supports the finite perfect algebraic quantum complex. Analytic quantization also requires propagators, renormalized products, and descent on the selected domains.

Gravity provides a separate physical test. The invertible coframe identifies flat Cartan solutions with Einstein solutions. A mixed boundary realization must additionally identify the boundary operations, their coherent bulk action, and the physical quantum currents. The later amplitude comparisons retain the state, pairing, and global geometric data required by each construction.
